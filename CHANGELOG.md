# Changelog

## 0.1.0

Initial public release.

- Six typed loaders: ECG, liquid capsule, fitness, orbit, pulse, and audio wave.
- Improved jumping jacks with articulated elbows, two jumps per cycle, grounded wide stance, softer landings, workout clothing details, and a responsive shadow.
- Inline, parent-filling, and native overlay presentation.
- Controlled progress, customizable colors, size, speed, text, and styles.
- Reduced-motion support, app-state pausing, and accessible progress values.
- Usage guide and an Expo playground in the source workspace.
